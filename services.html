Wall Types We Specialize In

Gravity (Concrete)

  • ✅ Cost-effective for small heights
  • ✅ Durable and long-lasting
  • ✅ Multiple finish options
  • ✅ Minimal maintenance

MSE (Mechanically Stabilized Earth)

  • ✅ Great for tall walls (2m+)
  • ✅ Flexible & seismic-resistant
  • ✅ Excellent load-bearing capacity
  • ✅ Cost-effective at height

Timber

  • ✅ Natural aesthetic appeal
  • ✅ Sustainable materials
  • ✅ Great for landscaping
  • ✅ Relatively affordable

Landscape/Stone

  • ✅ Beautiful natural look
  • ✅ Great for gardens
  • ✅ Various colors/styles
  • ✅ Design-focused

Hybrid

  • ✅ Best of both worlds
  • ✅ Versatile design
  • ✅ Custom solutions
  • ✅ Superior longevity

Other/Not Sure
