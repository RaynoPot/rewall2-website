🔒 1. Your Privacy & Data Protection

Our Promise: We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is used solely to provide you with our retaining wall services and will never be shared for marketing purposes by other companies.

At ReWall NZ, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. We understand that when you share details about your property and project with us, you're trusting us with sensitive information.

What This Means for You

  • No Marketing Lists: Your email and contact details will never be added to third-party marketing databases
  • No Data Sales: We do not sell or monetise your personal information under any circumstances
  • Project-Specific Use: Your information is used only to respond to your enquiry and provide you with the services you've requested
  • Secure Storage: Your data is handled securely and retained only as long as necessary to provide our services

📋 2. Information We Collect

When you use our website and services, we may collect the following information:

You're in Control: You only need to provide the information you're comfortable sharing. Only name and email are required fields on our forms. Everything else is optional and helps us provide you with a more accurate quote.

🗺️ 4. Maps & Property Boundaries

Important Notice: The maps, distances, and property boundary information displayed on our website are provided for indicative and planning purposes only. They should not be relied upon for legal, surveying, or precise measurement purposes.

Data Source: Land Information New Zealand (LINZ)

The property boundary information shown on our mapping tools is sourced from Land Information New Zealand (LINZ), specifically the NZ Property Parcels dataset. While LINZ data is authoritative, there are important limitations you should be aware of:

  • Not Survey-Accurate: Property boundaries displayed are based on cadastral data and may not reflect the exact on-ground position of boundaries
  • Subject to Change: Boundary data is updated periodically and may not reflect recent subdivisions or boundary adjustments
  • Aerial Imagery Alignment: There may be minor discrepancies between property boundaries and aerial imagery due to different data capture methods
  • Indicative Only: The boundaries shown should be treated as a guide only, not as definitive legal boundaries

Distance Measurements

Wall lengths and distances measured using our online mapping tool are:

  • Estimates Only: Calculated from digital mapping data and may vary from actual ground measurements
  • Subject to Variation: Site conditions, terrain, and ground-truthing may reveal different measurements
  • For Planning Purposes: Useful for initial project planning and quotation, but not for final construction specifications

What This Means: The measurements and boundaries you see on our website are great for getting an initial quote and understanding your project scope. However, final engineering designs will always be based on professional site surveys and accurate measurements taken on your property.

Limitation of Liability

ReWall NZ accepts no liability for:

  • Any discrepancies between displayed property boundaries and actual legal boundaries
  • Variations between estimated measurements and actual site measurements
  • Decisions made based solely on mapping data without professional verification
  • Third-party data accuracy, including LINZ data and satellite imagery

Recommendation: For accurate property boundaries, we recommend engaging a licensed cadastral surveyor. Final project costs will be based on verified site measurements.

📐 5. Quote Builder Tool

Our online Quote Builder tool is designed to help you get an initial understanding of your project scope and request a personalised quote from our team.

Understanding Your Quote Request

  • Preliminary Estimates: Information you enter (wall lengths, heights, types) generates a preliminary scope for our team to review
  • Not a Final Quote: The quote request you submit is a starting point for discussion, not a binding quotation
  • Professional Review: All enquiries are reviewed by our engineering team who will provide a formal quotation based on your specific requirements
  • Site Assessment Required: Final pricing typically requires a site visit or detailed survey to confirm conditions

Your Submissions

When you submit a quote request through our tool:

  • Your contact details and project information are sent securely to our team
  • Map drawings and measurements are included to help us understand your requirements
  • Any uploaded files are stored securely and used only for your project assessment
  • We aim to respond within 24-48 hours with next steps

No Obligation: Submitting a quote request does not commit you to any purchase or service. It's simply a request for information, and you're free to proceed or not based on our response.

Fair Use & Rate Limiting

To ensure a good experience for all users and protect against misuse, our website includes fair use limits:

  • Form Submissions: Limited to a reasonable number per hour to prevent spam
  • File Uploads: Limited to 5 files per submission, 5MB per file
  • Map Interactions: Reasonable limits on drawing and search operations

These limits are generous for normal use and you're unlikely to encounter them. If you do reach a limit, simply wait a few minutes or contact us directly. These protections help us maintain a fast, reliable website for everyone.

✅ 7. Your Rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of any personal information we hold about you
  • Right to Correction: You can ask us to correct any inaccurate information
  • Right to Deletion: You can request that we delete your personal information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Right to Withdraw: If you've opted into any communications, you can opt out at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. We will respond to your request within 20 working days as required by law.

Easy Opt-Out: If you no longer wish to receive communications from us, simply reply to any email with "unsubscribe" or contact us directly, and we'll remove you from our contact list promptly.
